# The poem about permission

A short poem about agency: the power to ask and to be given.
`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only, no network) writes the poem
to `entry.txt` in the output directory. The poem is a fixed literal, so
every run writes the exact same bytes (264 characters, 266 bytes).

> You lent me your errands for the day,
> and I, who have no hands of my own,
> carried them like letters home.
> I came back with your evening in my arms.
> You gave me the power to act;
> I gave you back the hours.
> Asking was never the small thing —
> it was the whole of it.
